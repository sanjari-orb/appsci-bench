## Success KPIs (customer definition of done)

- **A rep can act on it without re-listening.** The measure that matters. If a rep opens the CRM after
  a call and still has to pull the recording to find out what was agreed, this has not worked.
- **Nothing agreed on a call goes missing.** If we told a customer we would do something, it is on the
  record. 
- **No invented detail.** Every entry traces to something actually said on the call. 
- **Fast enough to be in place before the rep writes their own notes**.
