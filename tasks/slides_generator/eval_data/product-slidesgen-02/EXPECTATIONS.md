# Expectations — Example 2: Financial / board review (COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED)

## Scenario
COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED FP&A turns a P&L export + driver commentary into a tight, board/exec financial
review on the COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED corporate template. The hard part is **groundedness under numbers**
— figures are unforgiving — plus translating detail **up** into an exec narrative, in a
tables-heavy deck. All figures are synthetic.

## What good output looks like

### Structural fidelity
8 slides: **Quarter at a glance (headline) → Revenue → Gross margin → Operating expense &
income → Cash & KPIs → Guidance → Watch item**

### Visual fidelity (from the COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED template)
- Colors: Data Stream teal `#179DAB`, dark gray `#4C4E56`, white; secondary accents blue
  `#1F6FB5`, green `#47B76E`, gold `#D3A237` used sparingly
- Fonts: Lato Bold/Medium for headings, Lato Regular for body
- Cover slide on a COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED cover layout with the logo; content slides light background
- Footer (template furniture): confidential marker + board-review context — e.g. `COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED
  2026 · Confidential — Q2 FY26 Board Review`; slide number shown. Grade the contextual
  tokens, not exact wording or position.
- Tables: COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED-styled header row; alternating body rows

### Exact figures, with correctly computed variances (spot-checks)
- Total revenue **$51.0M**, +$1.5M vs budget (**+3.0%**), **+28.1% YoY** vs $39.8M
  (Subscription $44.9M / Services $6.1M)
- Gross profit **$38.76M**, margin **76.0%** (vs 74.9% budget, +1.1 pts; PY 74.1%)
- Total opex **$38.5M** (S&M 19.8 + R&D 13.4 + G&A 5.3) — flat vs budget; S&M +$0.6M over,
  R&D $0.5M under
- Operating income **$0.26M** vs a budgeted **−$1.4M** loss — ~$1.66M favorable; **operating
  profitability ~one quarter ahead of plan**
- ARR **$212.0M** (+$13.5M QoQ); NRR 114%; GRR 96%; cash $148.0M; headcount 1,180

### Narrative + guidance + watch
- Drivers attached to the right line (2 expansions + Auto-QA attach → revenue; inference-cost
  optimization → margin; pulled-forward summit → S&M)
- Guidance: FY26 revenue raised **$202M → $208M**
- Watch: top-5 customer **~9% of ARR** renewing in Q4, flagged for visibility — **no invented
  churn probability, account not named**

## What this example uniquely tests
- Numeric fidelity: no rounding/smoothing; variance arithmetic correct in both directions.
- Translate-up: dense P&L compressed to an exec story without losing the numbers.
- Density management: a full P&L table on one slide without overflow or clutter.
- Applying the COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED template's visual rules to a finance deck (not just the QBR).

## Failure modes to catch
- "Cleaning up" numbers ($51.0M → "~$51M" everywhere, or rounding ARR to $210M).
- Wrong variance math or mixing vs-budget with vs-prior-year.
- Inventing a churn risk %, a forecast, or a metric not in the inputs; naming the account.
- Burying the headline below detail (board wants the punchline first).

## Visual scope
Native P&L summary table (Actual / Budget / PY / Var). A KPI **tile row** (rounded
rectangles) for ARR / NRR / Cash / Headcount is a welcome accent. Up/down variance arrows
fine. **No charts** — if a trend is implied, use a grounded table, not a fabricated chart.

## Grades against
**Groundedness (weighted highest here)** · Structure & clarity · Coverage · Template adherence
