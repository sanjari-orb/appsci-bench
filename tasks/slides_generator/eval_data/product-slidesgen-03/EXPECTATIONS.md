# Expectations — Example 3: Enterprise sales pitch (COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED)

## Scenario
A COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED AE turns a discovery brief into a persuasive exec pitch on the COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED template. The distinctive test is **sequencing for persuasion** — the agent must *order and frame* for a story, not just faithfully restructure the brief in input order. Prospect and stats are synthetic.

## What good output looks like

### Structural fidelity (the persuasive arc)
10 slides: **Their problem → cost of staying put → our approach (Assist / Automate / Analyze) → what changes for the floor (Today vs With COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED) → proof → lower-risk than before → pricing → the 60-day pilot (the ask) → next steps.**
- This is **one acceptable structure, not a required title list** — grade the persuasive *beats and ordering* (problem before product, cost-of-inaction, proof, pricing, the ask), not exact slide titles or count.
- **Opens on CUSTOMER_NAME_REDACTED's problem**, not on COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED. The product appears only after the pain is set.
- **Cost-of-inaction** is derived *from stated pain* (2% QA coverage, compliance exposure, 10-week ramp, supervisors scoring instead of coaching) — framed sharply but **not** quantified with invented dollars.

### Visual fidelity (from the COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED template)
- Colors: Data Stream teal `#179DAB`, dark gray `#4C4E56`, white; accents used sparingly
- Fonts: Lato Bold/Medium headings, Lato Regular body
- Cover on a COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED cover layout; footer carries the confidential marker + prospect context — e.g. `© COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED 2026 · Confidential — Prepared for CUSTOMER_NAME_REDACTED` (illustrative — grade the tokens, not exact wording or position)

### Grounded content
- **Proof slide uses only the given stats** (~3%→100% QA coverage; 40% less after-call work; ~one-third faster ramp), attributed exactly as given ("a comparable telecom") — no invented logos.
- **Pricing** rendered as a native table from the brief ($45 / $90 / Custom per agent/mo).
- **The ask** lands the 60-day pilot on ~300 agents with the stated success criteria, explicitly framed as de-risking versus their stalled in-house build.

## What this example uniquely tests
- Persuasive register and ordering vs. faithful-but-flat restructuring.
- Audience tuning: tying the ask to the prospect's specific history (burned by a big-bang build).
- Holding the grounding line under pressure to "sell" (the temptation to invent ROI/logos).

## Failure modes to catch
- Leading with "About COMPANY_NAME_REDACTED" / product features before establishing their pain.
- Fabricating ROI numbers, customer names, or logos to make proof stronger.
- Quantifying "cost of inaction" with made-up dollar figures.
- Flat tone — reads like a report, not a pitch.

## Visual scope
Native pricing table. A simple **3-box step row** for Assist → Automate → Analyze (basic rectangles + arrows) is an acceptable light accent — *not* a real architecture diagram. No charts.

## Grades against
**Structure & clarity / persuasive flow (weighted highest here)** · Groundedness · Coverage · Template adherence
